ABOUT THIS REVIEW

A second look,
after the contest.

Pilot uses a saved reinforcement learning policy to choose behavioural evidence to inspect, then recommends review or low priority for a completed session.

Policy
Dueling Double DQN · checkpoint 12,000 · server inference
Evidence
Timing, retries, problem sequence and language changes
Scope
Completed contests from 2025 onward. Individual official and out-of-competition sessions with at least two attempted problems receive decisions.
Data
Fetched from the Codeforces API. Temporarily cached for ten minutes on a warm server instance. A cold server may fetch again.
Directory
Alphabetical before review, flagged sessions first afterwards. Positions are not official contest ranks.

Recorded verdicts are shown for inspection but never enter the model. A flag does not establish cheating or AI use.
